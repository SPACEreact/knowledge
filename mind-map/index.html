One connected system

Story is why. Craft is how it feels.

Move in order. Later domains borrow authority from earlier ones; technique becomes powerful only when it serves an emotional decision.

01 / FOUNDATION ↗

Storytelling

Character, conflict, transformation, pressure, and the emotional reason every visual decision exists.

 02 / FORM ↗

Design

Composition, colour, hierarchy, shape, space, material, and visual systems.

 03 / PERSPECTIVE ↗

Cinematography

Lens, light, camera height, movement, depth, and psychological point of view.

 04 / PRESENCE ↗

Sound

Space, texture, silence, rhythm, expectation, and what exists beyond the frame.

 05 / TIME ↗

Editing

Attention across duration: cuts, withholding, pulse, collision, and emotional continuity.

 06 / SYNTHESIS ↗

Emotion Grammar

Bring space, light, lens, movement, editing, sound, and texture together around the emotion you are engineering.

Five-minute practice

Two-second recall.

Look at any frame for two seconds. Hide it. Answer from memory—not analysis.

  2. 02What was the composition’s structural shape?
  3. 03What colour temperature dominated?
